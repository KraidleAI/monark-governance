# MONARK Dōjō: a signed daily record of MONARK holding

What this piece brings: for each day, the MONARK balances read on the public ledger for every holding address, a holding score per address computed by a published rule, and one root for the day, carried by a signed, chained line. Any holder can recompute their own score from the public ledger and check that their line is included under the day's root.

How it can be checked: the rule, the daily files and the public key are published with the piece, and a verifier recomputes the score and the inclusion proof offline.

This issue tracks the piece. It is closed when the piece ships in a tagged release. Questions and ideas are welcome in Discussions.
